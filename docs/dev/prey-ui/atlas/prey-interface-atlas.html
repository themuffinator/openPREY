Home screen

The front end is a view through a portal onto the Sphere over Earth. openPREY draws both bodies in 3D and opens close on the Sphere's shell, then swings round it and pulls back until the frame lays over the stock painting: the same Sphere, the same plates, and Earth's limb in front of the Sphere's foot as the stock layers sort it. Switch to the stock 2006 backdrop to compare. Pick an entry on the arc to watch its page grow out of the bottom bar; choose Return to Main Menu or press Escape to send it back.

The entrance

Out of game the menu opens 0.41 R above the Sphere's leading side, looking across its shell with the Sphere a little off-centre. Over 1200 ms the camera swings 38° round the Sphere on a curve while it pulls back, turning 35° to keep its aim, and comes to rest low over Earth. Speed peaks at 180 ms and falls away, and it is the Sphere's apparent radius that eases, so the move is quick at first and smooth to the end. Earth starts out of sight below; the camera crosses to Earth's side at 425 ms, and from 780 ms the limb rises into the frame in front of the Sphere. The frames show the backdrop alone.

Spec §2 · §13

Brand

Four registers carry the brand: space and the Sphere, the portals, the alien ship, and Tommy's Cherokee heritage. The wordmark belongs to the first two; the hawk Talon to the last.

Wordmark very wide extended capitals, dark fill, a thin #AFFCFF edge, and a blue lens streak (#3380B1, core near white) through the letters' middle. In the menu it fills a 476×37 u box at 100,55 u; the letters take the middle 60% of the height.

Talon the project emblem, drawn here as a fresh vector study. Spirit treatment: cyan light (#1BDDEF → #AFFCFF) on dark. Flame treatment: the application icon's red and orange with a cyan edge. It is the one warm element in project material.

Project name openPREY: "open" in a plain geometric sans, "PREY" in the extended face, never condensed. The game is "Prey" in running text.

  • Ground project material in space black with portal light; keep the hawk the only warm element.
  • Never condense "PREY", never set it in the body face, never recolour the hawk steel or olive.
  • Reconstruct the style; never reuse retail bitmaps. Quake 4 icons and splashes in assets/ are upstream material and never ship in an openPREY package.
Spec §4 · §10.2

Tokens

The front end's text and plate colours live in one stock file, guis/mainmenu/menucolors.guifragment; the rest are sampled from the interface art. The palette is cool from end to end: steel text that brightens toward white, slate glass, pale rails. Gold appears only on a hovered server-list heading.

Spec §5

Three faces, measured from glyph ink in the 48-point atlases and drawn here in openPREY's TrueType tracings of them. One textscale unit is a 48 u em. At the same scale the body face's capitals stand 1.8 times taller than the display face's. The ramp is drawn at 1 u = 1 px.

Spec §6 · Appendix B

Claw frame

Every frame is a black body with four mirrored claw corners and a 4 u edge strip carrying a 1.5 u rail top and bottom. There are no side rails: the body stops 25 u inside each end, so the claws stand clear of it. A frame no taller than its 32 u corner draws only its lower pair, which is how the home bottom bar gets claws at both ends.

Corner window_cornerUL, 128×128 texels drawn 38×32 u. Rim 7 texels #B3C6E2 (about 2 u); body #3B4351–#576272; oval socket 33×43 texels #171B20; beak along the outer edge joins the rail; foot curls inward.

Edges window_top stretched between corners 4 u tall, opaque for the top 49 of 128 texels: a 1.5 u rail. Mirrored along the bottom. Body: _black under matcolor 0.70 (0.50 on the home bottom bar and the Options page), inset by margins 25, 25, 1 or 7, 0.

Inside title bar 24 u tall at 32,8 and 64 u narrower than the frame (312 u here, 556 u on a full page), wider at the top; bottom title bar 19 u tall, wider at the foot. Children use the frame's own coordinates.

Spec §6 · §7

Plates

Buttons and setting rows have a different cut at each end and fade from the leading edge (0.77) to almost nothing (0.06); only their rims run the full length. Title and header bars are flat. Hover or focus a button: the graded fill becomes an even 0.30 and the label turns white. Nothing changes hue.

Button 3-slice button/corner · strip · invcorner, edge 12 u. Curved leading end, chamfered trailing end. #6F86A1. Label body face 0.33 (0.35 on New Game, Exit and Quit Game) #CCDEFA; hover white; unavailable #B5C9E3 at 0.30.

Setting row 25 u tall on a 30 u pitch. Slanted leading cut, curved trailing bracket, #546579. Label white 0.9 centred in the leading half; value #B8C9E6 0.9 centred in the trailing half, white on hover. Click a value to step it.

Header bar #111A25 at 0.60 with #6D7F95 rims; labels display face 0.45 #B3CCE6 0.9. Title bars #5C6C81 at 0.69, no rim. Picture frame 4–5 texel rounded stroke #A7C4E8.

Spec §10

HUD

No numbers. Tommy's silhouette fills with blood, his spirit twin stands behind him, ammo rises in a slim meter, and the weapon bar appears only while switching. Drive the gauges below; focus the stage and press 1–7, or use Switch weapon, to change weapons.

Figures health 35×90 u at 30,373, #990000 on #4D0000; spirit 7 u left and 4 u up, #4D66B3 on #001A4D. At 25 or less the health blinks on a 667 ms triangle; below 25 a hurt overlay joins it. Spirit walk swaps the figures over 700 ms and grades the world #80CCFF.

Ink #A0B2CA line art on dark fills. Ammo meter 21×100 u at 610,368, fill 8×66 u #8CA6CC 0.73; the foot dot pulses red every 333 ms when low; empty turns the frame and well red.

Weapon bar seven 70×60 u tiles from 75,5 sheared 0.22 forward; selected tile #4C5BD3 0.15 in a #748DA8 rim, its icon 50×28 → 60×35 u; visible 2 s then fading over 1 s. Pickups stack down the right edge, newest on top; damage arcs fade over 1 s. The crosshair inverts what lies behind it at 0.35.

Spec §10.8

Rifle scope

The Hunter rifle's scope is a living eye. Raising it grows a ring of instruments out of the centre of the view: three clamps, a compass of alien letters that turns with Tommy's heading, a hatched ring, a three-pointed reticle over a red glow, and a zoom scale on the leading side, all seen through a veined lens whose red tint runs in bands. Every size and colour here was measured from the stock overlay as openPREY draws it. Raise the scope, step the zoom and turn the heading below.

Aspect

Instrument centred on the screen and never stretched. Two ring lines at 165 and 175 u (#77211B, #7C2015); hatching from 120 u out to the inner line; compass letters 155–169 u, #812E1E at rest. Clamps at 0°, 120° and 240°: 17° sectors fading in from 112 u to a cap at 180–190 u, #743200 cooling to #631100. Reticle ring 17.5 u with three wedges from 26 u in to 10 u; glow 10 u #E80000.

Raising 0 ms the lens is there at once; the clamps grow from the centre to full size in 300 ms and cool over 1000 ms; the iris tint fades in over 400 ms; the zoom scale and pointer over 1000 ms; the text block over 2000 ms · 300 ms the compass grows in 300 ms, the rings and reticle in 200 ms, the glow fades in over 500 ms. Lowering removes everything at once.

Zoom field of view 5° to 40° in 5° steps, 20° at first, each step in 75 ms with weapon_rifle_scope_click. The scale is a crescent 238 u out, 2 u thick at its top and 14 u at its foot, from 44° below level to 44° above; the pointer rides it at 231 u, 43° below level at 40° and 45° above at 5°. The lens's veins are 0.3 opaque at 40° and fully opaque at 5°; its banded tint, 15 u to a cycle, is constant and scrolls a screen height a second (a tenth of that here). On a wide screen the lens covers the whole view; the instrument stays a circle.

Spec §11

Devices

The Sphere speaks a script Tommy cannot read until Talon translates it. Device screens are saturated and unstable, each family with its own palette. Land Talon on the console below to translate it; the overlay stack can be switched layer by layer.

translationAlpha 0.00 · linear over 1000 ms from its current value · console_generic_translation on landing only · the notranslation 1 digit columns fade and get no English.

Frame one white half panel at 10,10,310,460 and again mirrored at 320,10,−310,460, tinted #80CCFF 0.7. Stack scanlines 0.05 scrolling up · roll bar #B3804D every 5 s · flicker 0.05–0.25 on a 500 ms cycle · static 0.02 · damage static 0.2 and V-hold 0.4. The stage draws the scanlines at seven times and the static at two and a half times stock strength so that they read at this size.

The reveal as the retail engine draws it, and as openPREY now does: each alien-face window fades by 1 − translationAlpha, shadow included; the same string in the body face appears 6 u higher at translationAlpha, without a shadow; a notranslation 1 window only fades. The digit column is an example.

Loading and credits

Loading shows the level's art across the whole screen, its name in the display face on a black band, and a claw-ended progress bar. In the credits, title lines fly in from the trailing side while sheared, then straighten; names appear in place.

Loading name band 640×35 u at 0,10, black 0.55; name display face 0.6 #A6BFFF 0.8; author line 0.3 #D9D9FF 0.75 (openPREY). Progress fill 256×12 u at 192,455, #5959FF 0.65, in the bottombar outline 304×30 u at 168,443.

Credits title lines start at x 1000 u sheared 0.6, reach x 150 u in 500 ms, swing to the opposite shear by 500 ms and straighten by 600 ms; names appear in place. Titles #D1E3FC, names #99ADC7 0.88, headings #CCDEFA. The page scrolls 5300 u in 130 s.
