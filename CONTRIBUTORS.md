# openPREY contributors

- **[themuffinator](https://github.com/themuffinator)** — project author and maintainer, DarkMatter Productions; author of openQ4, from which the openPREY engine is merged.
- **[Emile Belanger (emileb)](https://github.com/emileb)** — original Android platform port and SigmaTouch bridge; OpenGL ES 3.0 renderer and specialized shader variants; ETC2/EAC texture compression; mobile loading, cache, memory and audio improvements. His [Android](https://github.com/emileb/openQ4/tree/android) and [GLES shader-variants](https://github.com/emileb/openQ4/tree/gles-shader-variants) branches are the source of this contribution, adapted to current official openQ4 and carried into openPREY with its engine. See the [integration record](docs/dev/android-gles-integration.md) for exact revisions and adaptations.
- **[Ronald Rivest and RSA Data Security, Inc.](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1320)** — the native and Android PK4 compatibility checksums are derived from the RSA Data Security, Inc. MD4 Message-Digest Algorithm. Original notices are retained in the implementations and [MD4 notice](LICENSES/MD4.txt).

The [README credits](README.md#credits) acknowledge the upstream projects, source authors, libraries and testing contributions on which openPREY depends. Original source notices remain in their files.
